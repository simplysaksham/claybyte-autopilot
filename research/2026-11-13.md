## Topic research: client feedback / approval windows
- Agency Management Institute sample SOW clause: "three (3) business days" to review; after that "it shall be deemed that the Client has reviewed and approved the work". https://agencymanagementinstitute.com/do-you-have-a-feedback-approval-clause-in-your-sows/
- Enterprise DNA (Oct 12, 2026, vendor): "Don't treat an unanswered reminder as approval"; an overdue decision should "surface a choice: approve by a new time, move the delivery date, or reduce the scope"; one decision-maker with a "named backup"; the account owner "consolidates feedback before work resumes". https://enterprisedna.co/resources/guides/agencies-stop-client-approval-delays
- Freelancers Union (2014): put the feedback timetable in the delivery terms; "the new deadline will be X"; rush rates if they're late. https://blog.freelancersunion.org/2014/02/19/what-do-your-client-slow-respond/
