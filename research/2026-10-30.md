## Carousel design
- Cover headline ≤ 8 words; bold condensed type; never mid-on-mid contrast; keep text in the top ~60%; max 2 design elements; clear swipe cue (arrow, cut-off element, "Swipe →"). https://carouselli.com/blog/instagram-carousel-cover-image, https://resont.com/blog/top-instagram-carousel-hooks/
- Consistent grid, fonts, colors and logo corner across slides; progress indicators; hierarchy roughly 4 : 3 : 1.5. https://pineable.com/blog/social-media-carousel-design-best-practices
- Structure hook → problem → core → recap → CTA. https://www.brandghost.ai/blog/posts/instagram-carousel-design-guide
- 8–12 slides for educational, 3–5 for quick tips. https://www.krumzi.com/blog/15-instagram-carousel-ideas-that-actually-drive-engagement-in-2026

## Reels
- Short proof reels (6–18 s) and longer process videos (40 s–2 min) win; generic middle underperforms. AI-tool content leads with the result. Comment-keyword CTAs work best after the payoff. https://lightreel.ai/blogs/whats-trending-on-instagram
- Educational save-led reel: problem → 3–5 one-line points → save CTA. https://insights.vaizle.com/instagram-reel-trends/
- Silent text-overlay storytelling with 2–5 s cuts; text in the top half. Overused: expectation vs reality, day-in-the-life, green-screen reactions. https://slidycreator.com/blog/instagram-reel-trends-2026/
- Hook in the first 1–2 s; shorter is better; reshares and time spent weighted more since mid-2026; trial reels test with non-followers; avoid watermarks. https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels/
