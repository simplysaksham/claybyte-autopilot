## Deposits (verified, vendor guides)
- Penly (Jun 18, 2026): "The standard is 25–50%… For smaller projects (under $500), 50% is common. For larger engagements, 25–33% is typical." Ask in the proposal. https://penly.it.com/blog/how-to-collect-a-deposit-from-freelance-clients
- Ruul (Jul 2026): 25–50%; full upfront for "small, well-defined digital deliverables"; milestones for long projects, e.g. "30% upfront, 30% at a defined midpoint and 40% on delivery"; "never carry more than one phase of work without payment"; state terms as fact, not "Would you be okay if…". https://ruul.io/blog/freelance-payment-terms
