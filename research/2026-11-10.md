## Trends and algorithm
- Mean CEO (Nov 1, 2026): saves, shares, story replies, profile visits and DM sends are the "private signals" that matter more than likes; ~70% of views land in the first 72 h (Metricool, second-hand). Reel styles that work now: tiny-text, story-led, **ranking**, list, green-screen commentary, voiceover B-roll. Hooks should be specific and outcome-led ("3 reasons your content gets views but no leads"). https://blog.mean.ceo/instagram-trends-november-2026/
- Pulse Advertising (updated Jul 2026): ranking weights DM shares, saves, watch time and profile clicks; Instagram's AI reads on-screen text and voiceover, so on-screen text should use the words people search. Unsourced weights ("one DM share ≈ 15 likes"), not used in posts. https://www.pulse-advertising.com/resources/social-media-news/instagram-algorithm-changes-2026/

## Carousel design
- CreatorFlow (updated Oct 30, 2026): cover has ~2 s and ~10 words; three shapes: specific claim, named mistake, before/after. Slide 2 should show the payoff early, never restate slide 1. "One job per slide", every slide must read on its own. Last slide = one plain ask. Cites Socialinsider (Oct 2026): carousels 0.50% engagement in Q3 vs reels 0.48% (second-hand). https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-carousel-structure/
- PostNitro (Mar 2026): an arrow/graphic that **bleeds off the right edge into the next slide**; 1–2 sentences per slide; CTA slide visually distinct. https://postnitro.ai/blog/post/instagram-carousel-design

## Money research (today's carousel)
- Influish (2026, India-focused): ad rights = 20–50% of the content fee per month, up to 100%+ for broad scope; examples 30 days ≈ 30%, 90 days multi-platform ≈ 80%, 1 year all platforms ≈ 100%. Put exact platforms, duration, placements, exclusivity, renewal in writing; avoid "unlimited usage". https://www.influish.com/blog/how-much-should-influencers-charge-for-ad-rights-in-2026
- Influencer Marketing Hub: whitelisting (ads served from the creator's handle) ≈ 20–25% of the base rate per 30 days; clause should fix the period and fee and forbid edits without approval. https://influencermarketinghub.com/whitelisting-and-spark-ads/
